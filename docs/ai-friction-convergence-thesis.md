## 1. Thesis (the invariant)

The market prices AI adoption as a smooth, durable productivity re-rating. The trench-level
truth is that **"it works" means "it demoed," not "it holds"**: the marketed productivity gains
are largely demo-grade, front-loaded, and convert into deferred, correlated, system-wide
fragility. The market is long the marketing; **we are long the gap** between the demo and the
load. Recognition of that gap is inevitable but path-free, and every route lands in the same
place: the marketed number gets discounted and an AI governance / cleanup layer becomes
mandatory spend.

## 2. The edge is tacit knowledge, and why it is durable

- **The edge:** distinguishing demo from real productivity gain. Requires hundreds of hours in
  the trenches of AI-driven ("PDE") orgs. From inside, the gap is obvious; from outside,
  invisible.
- **Why the market can't see it (captured oracle):** the market's only interpreters of whether
  AI works — the vendor, the analyst, the internal champion, the consultant, "the sharp you pay
  to cut the fat" — are all incentivized to the enthusiast, one-more-tweak narrative. Their
  survival depends on it. The people who actually know are silenced, inarticulate, or on the
  payroll. The correction mechanism is captured, so the mispricing is structural and durable, not
  a transient inefficiency.
- **Why it survives being written down:** tacit knowledge does not transfer on the page. The
  market can read this document and still not hold the fuse-length feel, so publication does not
  arbitrage it away. The explicit argument is free; the tacit read is the moat.
- **The enthusiast–doom divide IS the mispricing (added 2026-11-02).** Both loud camps argue about
  *raw* inference and miss the trench middle:
  - *Enthusiasts:* "AI is awesome — I have 17 agents running 24/7." (Long the marketing; rung-1
    maximalists.)
  - *Doom / naysayers:* "look how dumb it is, it can't even spell strawberry." (Short the capability.)
  - *The silent middle (the edge):* you give the model **a tool that spells strawberry** (the
    grounding / connector layer) **and a verification step on the output** (the governance / check —
    "click twice, wait for Jim"). The friction — the tool and the check — is the load-bearing part
    neither camp prices.

  This is the captured oracle made concrete: the two loudest interpreters are split between hype and
  mockery of *raw* inference, and the quiet operators who know the fix (tool + verify) have no
  megaphone. The example maps the basket exactly — the **tool** = grounding (§6 rung 2), the
  **verification** = governance / cleanup (§6 rung 3). We are long both.

## 3. Policy-family placement

theta runs two policy families:

- **Cheap convex (the wrapper / theta's "ETF"):** the honest no-tacit-knowledge default.
  Mechanical, high-cadence, breadth over conviction; harvests commoditized signal with a
  latency/execution edge. Judged like an index fund; earns its default status only if it clears
  its cost stack net.
- **Human thesis (this doc):** rare, concentrated, conviction-held, licensed only by nameable
  tacit knowledge. The harness never holds the thesis — it holds execution and a pre-committed
  exit (the discipline). The human holds the knowledge (the edge).

Gate between families: can we name the specific tacit knowledge that gives us the side the
market can't take? For this thesis: yes (Section 2). A human-thesis-**sized** bet on
cheap-convex-**grade** knowledge is the lethal zone — do not cross it.

## 4. Epistemic frame

- **Efficiency → wrapper.** A hyper-efficient market gives no predictive edge, so you wrap
  (harvest structure), agnostic by design.
- **Convergence → efficiency breaks.** Efficiency is a property of diversity, not speed. When
  everyone runs the same model/signal, the market is not faster-efficient — it is a herd:
  predictable and fragile in a direction. The force that removes the friction removes the other
  side of the book. The moment there is no other side, there is a side to take.
- **Convergent validity.** Multiple independent angles land on the same trade (triangulation).
  Valid only if the legs have different failure conditions — else it is one belief in four
  costumes (the herd's own error). The structural legs (microstructure, efficiency-breakdown)
  survive even if the AI-fundamentals leg is wrong, because any monoculture produces them
  (portfolio insurance '87, correlated VaR '08, passive flows now). That is what makes it more
  than nothing.

*Forcing function (money math, late 2026).* OpenAI ~$50B annualized revenue (Sep 2026) against
~$1.4T/8yr compute commitments (Altman, 30GW); the traceable disclosed contracts ~$720B (Oracle
$300B, MSFT $250B, AWS $138B, CoreWeave, Cerebras) imply ~$117–136B/yr compute = **2.3–2.7×

Google-search scale — unreachable via labeled banners, reachable only by monetizing intent at the
decision point (the answer). **Monetizing the answer is scheduled, not optional.**

*The firewall is definitional, not substantive.* OpenAI's rule — "ads do not influence ChatGPT's
answers" — fences off only the model's text, not the funnel it feeds. Already live: advertiser
**bids rank which ad shows** (pay-to-rank, inside the "ad system"); **Sponsored Agents** move one
click past the clean answer into a business-controlled sales conversation; **product discovery**
surfaces catalog items with OpenAI itself drawing the organic-vs-paid line (a dial it owns);
Instant Checkout launched with a real undisclosed take-rate, then "pulled back" to merchant-site
checkout (iterating the meter, not removing it). So **"answer independence" ≠ "no commercial
influence over what you see/buy."** It is the consent-checkbox of the recommendation economy:
technically true, substantively porous, doing PR/regulatory work while extraction relocates to
where the boundary isn't. Erosion path: commercial weight folds into "relevance," organic product
recs get a bid-influenced rank framed as a product improvement (the Google creep), each step
individually defensible — the paper trail patched over like Billy.

*Why there is no governor (ties to the selective-brake note).* The harm — a recommendation
slightly more shill than best — is **diffuse, unattributable, invisible**, so regulation is
outrun by construction (the brake bites only on salient/attributable harm, e.g. the companion-bot
case). The trust brake fails too: the **fluency heuristic** makes best-pick, corpus-default, and
highest-bidder sound identical, so users can't tell and can't defect. No legal brake, no trust
brake, a ~$86B hole, a $100B prize → **maximal monetization is structurally forced**, not a
choice OpenAI could decline.

*Investable read.* OpenAI private → direct winner diluted (SoftBank proxy). Public expression is
the migration: the $100B ad pool is carved substantially from **Google's search** hide (sharpest
GOOGL swing-toward-short + the open-web short); rails **V/MA** clip the transactions it drives;
placement-winners + **AEO tools (ADBE/HUBS)** sell the shovels. **Catalyst to hawk (now
concrete):** the first sponsored product recommendation blended into organic results, or a
take-rate reinstated at scale — the boundary moving = corpus-incumbency and placement get repriced.

## 10. Weighting methodology (v2 — pressure-tested 2026-11-02)

Owner: lead developer / desk · Agent ID: research-agent (Claude Opus 4.8)
Status: DESIGN SPEC — pressure-tested; not built, not armed, nothing traded. Capex-term settled 2026-11-02 (§10.4).

The bridge from the §6 player list to a held basket. Pressure-tested against a senior
portfolio-construction peer (scoring, weighting engine, rebalance); this is the surviving design.

**Keep it minimal (design law).** The edge is the human tacit scores, not the machinery. The engine
is deliberately four moving parts: a human score, a bounded tilt, a capped weighting, a lazy
tax-aware rebalance. Anything fancier — Black-Litterman, mean-variance optimization, factor models,
logistic/z-score penalties, a measured "oracle" composite — stays OUT until a live result proves the
simple version is leaving money on the table. Overengineering is the easy failure here;
friction-is-load-bearing applies to our own code too.

### 10.1 Two human inputs (the seed)

Scored by trench knowledge, never by a model (Core Architecture Law). Per name:

- **`friction` (0–10)** — how un-bypassable the company is when AI cracks under load:

| Score | Meaning | Trench template |
|---|---|---|
| 9–10 | Un-bypassable mandatory spend | Fundamental infra / audit / cleanup that MUST deploy when a system cracks under load; the architecture can't survive the gap without them. |
| 7–8 | High-friction structural guardrail | Critical governance/security that mitigates deferred fragility, but orgs may try to bypass it during the hype/demo phase before reality hits. |
| 4–6 | Neutral / superficial utility | Sits in the governance space but is a marketing wrapper itself; sells a checklist, doesn't fix trench-level fragility. |
| 2–3 | High-fragility risk exposure | Core workflows / products built on demo-grade, fragile AI scaffolding; actively introducing systemic technical debt. |
| 0–1 | Extreme fragility (the core delta) | Firing engineers because "the demo worked"; entirely long the marketing, maximal producer of future systemic failure. |

- **`oracle` (0–10)** — how hard the Street already buys the company's AI-marketing narrative. Stays
  a human score (the edge is tacit); no measured composite is built unless the simple score proves
  unreliable. Higher oracle = more discount in 10.2 (we favor the un-bypassable name the market is
  *ignoring*):

| Score | `oracle` — how hard the Street already buys the AI-marketing narrative |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | The name **IS** the consensus AI trade — max adoration, story fully priced, valuation embeds the narrative. |
| 7–8 | Heavily-owned, widely-cited AI winner; narrative largely priced, some debate left. |
| 4–6 | AI angle recognized but contested or secondary to the core business; mixed Street view. |
| 2–3 | AI relevance under-appreciated; Street still treats it as a non-AI name. |
| 0–1 | Off the AI radar — no narrative premium; the market doesn't connect it to the thesis. |

Scoring: one lane at a time for calibration; score `friction` and `oracle` independently; low scores
must mean a genuine read, not sparse coverage. Seed sheet: `docs/weighting-seed.csv` (207 names,
objective columns pre-filled). Ranked output: `docs/weighting-ranked.csv`; selected book:
`docs/weighting-basket.csv` (38 names on strawman scores).

### 10.2 Score (bounded — friction leads)

```
score = friction × (1 − 0.5 · oracle/10)
```

- The **napkin inverse** `10/(oracle+0.1)` is rejected: it gave ignored names up to a ~100×
  multiplier, ranking low-friction/ignored above high-friction/covered (inverting our thesis) and
  rewarding illiquid obscurity (fighting the liquidity gate).
- The oracle term is a **bounded discount** (max consensus shaves ≤50%, never flips a 10 below a 2):
  contrarianism tilts, it can't dominate.
- **Crowding is NOT in the score** (settled 2026-11-02). Conviction lives here (friction +
  contrarian tilt); crowding / correlation is a portfolio constraint, handled by the crowded-capex
  bucket cap in 10.4. Keeping them separate avoids double-counting and keeps the score about one
  thing — how much we believe the name.

Sanity check, not a subsystem: eyeball whether a ±1-point score change swings a name's weight a lot
— if so it's noise, size it down.

### 10.3 Pipeline

```
§6 catalogue → liquidity/handle gate → conviction selection → score → capped weighting → hold/rebalance
```

1. **Gate.** Drop no-handle / private; require > $5M/day dollar volume; US-listed / ADR only (cash
   account).
2. **Select — forced, not optional.** Feasibility needs `N·floor ≤ 1 ≤ N·cap`; at floor 2% / cap
   20%, **N ∈ [5, 50]**. The 130-name catalogue can't be held (130 × 2% = 260%). Cut to ~40–50 by
   conviction first; the floor is a *selection* rule, never inside the weight loop (it oscillates
   there).
3. **Score.** Per 10.2, on the kept set only (excluded names leave the denominator).
4. **Weight.** Capped water-fill: `w = min(cap, μ · score)`, μ set so Σw = 1 (standard capped-index
   math — solve directly, not a hand-rolled loop). Then group caps (10.4).
5. **Hold / rebalance.** Per 10.5.

### 10.4 Caps (the only risk control we start with)

- **Single-name cap** (~20%), and a **min trade size** instead of a hard weight floor (a floor
  forces capital into the weakest names and breaks feasibility).
- **Zone cap** — our §6 zones *are* the sectors; cap each so one correlated bucket can't become the
  book. (The lanes are our poor-man's covariance; no correlation matrix.)
- **Crowded-capex bucket cap (settled 2026-11-02).** Cap the portfolio's crowded exposure:
  `Σ(capex_beta_i · w_i) ≤ ~20%` (tunable down), where `capex_beta ∈ {0 edge-pure, 0.5 mixed,
  1 crowded}` from the §6 tag — edge-pure contributes nothing, mixed counts half. One constraint,
  same family as the zone caps. Chosen over a score discount (don't double-count crowding) and over
  doing both (don't overengineer): the cap directly bounds the risk we actually care about — the
  basket quietly becoming the crowded consensus long.
- Monitor effective-N. That's it — add more only if a live result demands it.

### 10.5 Rebalance (taxable cash account, hold mandate)

- **Drift is an alert, not an auto-reset.** `Σ|w − w_target| > 0.10` → review, don't "trade back to
  target" (that realizes gains the hold mandate exists to defer). Fill underweights with new cash /
  dividends first.
- **Asymmetric** — don't trim a winner for being over target (that sells strength); trim only at the
  hard cap (back to a buffer, e.g. 18%, not to target), a thesis break, or a risk limit.
- **Tax-lot aware** (tax drag = a §5 bleed): specific-lot ID, sell long-term / highest-basis first,
  avoid short-term gains, no wash-sale rebuy inside the 30-day window.

- [ ] Selection rule (top-N by score? a hard friction floor, e.g. keep ≥ 7?).
- [ ] Placeholders to set: cap 20% / min trade size / drift 0.10 / buffer 18% / oracle λ 0.5.

## 11. Live expression — the harness is the ETF wrapper

Owner: lead developer / desk · Agent ID: research-agent (Claude Opus 4.8)
Status: DESIGN SPEC — not built, not armed, nothing traded. The §10 weights are the product; this is how we *hold* them.

**Robinhood has no build-your-own-ETF** (confirmed against Robinhood's own docs, 2026-11-02:
fractional/dollar shares yes, recurring buys into one ticker yes, recurring across multiple coins
crypto-only — but no native "define target weights across N stocks, auto-allocate deposits, rebalance
to weights" product; that is an M1-pie / Fidelity-Basket / Schwab-Personalized-Indexing feature
Robinhood does not carry). There is no Robinhood "ETF pool" object to deposit into — and we do not
need one. **The harness is the wrapper.** Our ETF is a set of durable equity positions plus a
rebalance rule; no fund, no ticker, no 40-Act sponsor, no wrapper fee. This is the right amount of
code: almost all of it already exists on the equity spine, and the one new lane is thin.

### 11.1 What we hold

N durable **notional** equity positions in the pinned agentic cash account (872449178), weighted by
§10, *held* — not timed (§5 path-independence). The "ETF" is the positions + the rebalance rule.
Long-only, cash-account, settled-cash-gated (Capital Firewall). The operator funding the account is
the only contribution surface; the engine does the allocation.

### 11.2 The basket spec — reuse the ThesisSpec (don't invent)

Each basket slot is one `ThesisSpecLeg` (`app/Robinhood/Thesis/ThesisSpecLeg.php`):

- `asset_class: equity`, `direction: long`, `sizing.mode: balance_fraction` — **the §10 weight is the
  fraction**. `SIZING_BALANCE_FRACTION` ("a fraction of settled balance — the size scales with the
  account") already exists; it is the ETF weight by construction, so the book re-sizes itself as the
  account grows with deposits.
- `leg_id` → the durable `logical_order_id` for that slot.
- `invalidation.thesis_stop` only (we hold unless the *thesis* breaks); no `time_stop`, no
  per-leg price stop unless a name earns one. This is the opposite of the options lane's fast-exit.

The 38-name basket drops straight in as 38 balance-fraction legs. `ThesisSpec` / `ThesisSpecLeg` /
`ThesisSpecValidator` already parse and validate this document — reused, not rebuilt.

### 11.3 The order primitive — reuse the equity spine

- **Fractional is already wired:** `EquityExecutor::orderPayload()`
  (`app/Robinhood/Execution/EquityExecutor.php`) emits Robinhood's `dollar_amount` field — a notional
  order = fractional shares. A $180 slot in a $5k book is a dollar amount, not a whole-share problem.
- **Durable, non-double-submitting:** `OrderIntent::intentKey()` with a `logical_order_id` hashes to
  `durable|strategy|logical_id`; a rebalance pass reconciles the same slot instead of re-buying it.
- Rails (`RailPipeline`), ref-before-place (`OrderRefStore`), the journal
  (`RobinhoodTradeJournal`), and equity reconciliation are all already on this path.
- **The options thesis lane (`theta:thesis-assemble` / `theta:thesis-execute`) is the amplifier — a
  different lane.** The basket reuses the shared spine (rails, refs, executor, journal) and reuses
  *none* of the options machinery (DTE bands, chase ladder, entry window, per-play debit). Wrong tool
  for a hold-forever equity sleeve.

### 11.4 The rebalance engine — `theta:basket-rebalance` (the one new command)

Deterministic diff, no model on the path:

```
read broker truth:  settled_cash, equity positions   (get_portfolio / get_equity_positions — NOT journal intent)
per name i:         target_notional_i = w_i × (settled_cash + basket_MV)
                    drift_i = target_notional_i − current_notional_i
emit:               bounded marketable-limit NOTIONAL buys to close positive drift
```

**Deposit → water-fill falls out for free.** A deposit into 872449178 lands as settled cash; the next
pass sees cash > 0 and spreads it across the basket by weight — pure buys, no timing, no selling.
That is the "add to the pool" behavior the operator wants, and it *is* the §5 invariant (hold,
don't time, path-independent). Selling is governed by §10.5 only (drift alert, asymmetric, tax-lot
aware), never by the deposit path. Gates carried, not re-invented: fractionability (§11.6),
settled-cash-only (no unsettled-proceeds recycling), long-only, §10.4 caps, kill switch.

### 11.5 The rebalance ledger — the ceremony (new primitive)

A rebalance is a **first-class durable entity**; every name it touches is a **line item**; the whole
pass reconciles against broker truth. Two tables, parent/child:

```
rebalance_event (parent — one row per pass)
  id, owner, agent_id                         ← artifact-ownership law
  trigger        deposit | drift | score_change | manual | scheduled
  as_of
  — broker-truth snapshot at open —
  settled_cash_before, basket_mv_before, book_before, deposit_detected
  — target source (reproducible) —
  thesis_spec_id, spec_version, weights_payload_hash
  — outcome totals —
  n_legs, gross_buys_usd, gross_sells_usd, cash_deployed, settled_cash_after
  status         planned → placing → placed → reconciled | partially_filled | aborted
```

```
rebalance_leg (line items — one row per name touched)
  rebalance_event_id (FK), ticker, logical_order_id
  weight_target, notional_target, position_before (shares + MV, broker truth), drift_usd
  action         buy | sell | hold | drop
  reason         deposit_fill | drift_trim | new_admit | dropped_untradable | at_target
  fractionable   gate result (§11.6)
  — order/fill truth BY REFERENCE —
  client_ref_id, broker_order_id  → the RobinhoodTradeJournal row
```

**Reuse boundary.** The journal stays the per-order broker-truth record (shared with the options
lane): fill truth — `filled_qty`, `fill_price`, `filled_at`, status per the `LIVE_FILLED` /
`LIVE_POSTED_NOT_FILLED` / … taxonomy — lives on the journal row. The `rebalance_leg` adds only the
basket context (target weight, drift, reason) and points at the journal by `client_ref_id`. No
duplicated fill columns. **Structured rows are the source of truth; any Markdown/CSV readback is
rendered from them** (ledger law — no hand-maintained Markdown ledger).

**Reconciliation closes the pass.** Read broker positions back, write `settled_cash_after` and each
leg's journal fill truth. A leg whose journal row never reaches `LIVE_FILLED` stays open: the next
pass re-derives the diff from *live* positions, so an unfilled buy is simply re-attempted — never
double-counted, because the `logical_order_id` / ref makes it idempotent. A deposit is just the
history of `deposit_detected` across events; the audit trail answers "what changed, when, triggered
by what, and did the broker confirm it."

### 11.6 Fractionability gate — the one real constraint

Notional orders cover most large-cap US equities and many ETFs, but **not** ADRs reliably, OTC names,
or thinner closed-end funds (DXYZ, BST/BSTZ sit here). Holdability (`handle ∈ {public, ADR, OTC,
fund}`) is the §10.3 gate; **notional-tradability is stricter and per-name** (`get_equity_tradability`
/ the review call). A name that fails → whole-share sizing (may not fit a small book) or
`dropped_untradable`, recorded as a leg reason, never silently skipped. This gate belongs in the
command, not the scoring sheet.

### 11.7 Built vs. gap

- **Reused (already built):** `ThesisSpec` / `ThesisSpecLeg` / `ThesisSpecValidator`,
  `balance_fraction` sizing, `EquityExecutor` + `dollar_amount`, `OrderIntent` durable key,
  `RailPipeline`, `OrderRefStore`, `RobinhoodTradeJournal`, equity reconciliation.
- **New (to build):** `theta:basket-rebalance` (diff + notional buys + fractionability gate);
  `rebalance_event` + `rebalance_leg` models / migrations / relations; a thin weights source
  (`weighting-basket.csv` → `balance_fraction` legs, or author the spec directly).
- **Not reused:** the options amplifier's DTE / chase / window / per-play-debit machinery — wrong lane.

Sizing is notional-agnostic: the §10 weights *are* the allocation; dollars = weight × settled
balance. "Less than $1M initially" changes nothing about the design — only fractionability at the
very small end.
